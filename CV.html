Lionel Andrés Messi

Profession: Professional Footballer

Nationality: Argentina

Date of Birth: 24 June 1987

Position: Forward

Current Club: Inter Miami CF

National Team: Argentina

Professional Profile

Lionel Andrés Messi is widely regarded as one of the greatest football players in football history. He is known for his exceptional dribbling, passing, vision, creativity, finishing, and leadership. Throughout his career, he has won numerous domestic and international trophies while inspiring millions of football fans around the world.

Career History

  • Newell's Old Boys Youth Academy (1994 to 2000)
  • FC Barcelona Youth Academy - La Masia (2000 to 2004)
  • FC Barcelona (2004 to 2021)
  • Paris Saint-Germain (2021 to 2023)
  • Inter Miami CF (2023 to Present)

Major Achievements

  • FIFA World Cup Champion (2022)

  • Multiple La Liga Champion
